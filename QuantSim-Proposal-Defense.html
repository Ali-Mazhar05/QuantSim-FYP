01Project Overview
Simulation is the core; everything else is instrumentation around it
Principle P1. The market environment — replayed-historical or synthetic — is the substrate the trading engine, ML, stress testing, and TDA all consume through one common interface. Three analytical approaches quantify portfolio risk over that shared environment.
MARKET ENVIRONMENT · replayed-historical or synthetic — bound through one common interface (FR-MKT-009)
▲        ▲        ▲

🛡️Stress Testing

Inject designed catastrophes — flash-crash and volatility-spike (Core) — and report maximum drawdown, VaR, recovery days, and each instrument's contribution to the loss.

🔗Topological Data Analysis

The principal novelty. Studies the shape of the market's correlation structure to detect regime transitions that conventional indicators miss.

🤖Machine Learning

Volatility forecasts, sentiment, and strategy metrics — every prediction tied to a registered model version and built on look-ahead-safe features.

🔎 Explainable — SHAP attributions + plain-language narration (M6) ⚙️ Eight services on an Apache Kafka event backbone 💻 Demonstrable end-to-end on the local Docker Compose stack
02Motivation
The failures that happen silently

🕳️Look-ahead bias

Financial ML fails silently under look-ahead bias — a model trained on data it could not have known at prediction time. QuantSim's point-in-time feature store makes this structurally impossible, not a discipline the modeller must remember.

📦Black-box outputs

Most platforms emit a risk number with no reasoning. The premise here is that no analytical output is a black box: SHAP attributions plus a plain-language narration accompany every prediction, alert, and stress result.

📈Backtests that overstate returns

A backtest that ignores transaction costs and slippage overstates returns. Realistic execution is required, not optional — configurable cost and slippage models apply to every fill.

🔗Transitions the indicators miss

Volatility and correlation spike during a crash, not before it. Topological Data Analysis studies the shape of the market's correlation structure to detect regime transitions those indicators miss — the platform's principal novelty.

03Literature Review & Research Analysis
Built on established research, aimed at an open gap

A · TDA in Finance

Gidea & Katz (2018) show persistent-homology landscapes gave early-warning signals before the 2000 & 2008 crashes; Gidea (2017) studies topology of critical transitions in financial networks.

B · Topology Foundations

Carlsson (2009) "Topology and Data"; Edelsbrunner et al. (2002) topological persistence; Bubenik (2015) persistence landscapes; Bauer (2021) the Ripser algorithm.

C · Stochastic Modeling

Black–Scholes (1973) & Merton (1976, jumps); Uhlenbeck–Ornstein (1930, mean reversion); Hamilton (1989, regime-switching); Glasserman (2003, Monte Carlo).

D · Risk, ML & Explainability

Artzner et al. (1999) coherent risk; Rockafellar–Uryasev (2000) CVaR; Bao et al. (2017) LSTM forecasting; Lundberg–Lee (2017) SHAP; Bailey et al. (2014) / López de Prado (2018) on backtest overfitting.

The gap → TDA early-warning is promising but lives mostly in offline academic studies; risk platforms remain late & opaque; and backtests routinely leak look-ahead bias. No system operationalizes topology-aware, explainable, look-ahead-safe risk analytics end-to-end. That is where QuantSim sits.

04High-Level Goals
Key objectives & outcomes

01 Grounded simulated markets

Calibrate four stochastic models — GBM, mean-reversion, jump-diffusion, regime-switching — to real data and generate Monte Carlo path ensembles.

02 Three analytical approaches

Stress testing, Topological Data Analysis, and machine learning — all over one shared market environment.

03 Operational TDA

A composite topological-risk score per analysis window, plus flagged regime transitions between consecutive windows.

04 Explainable by default

SHAP attributions and a plain-language narration for every prediction, alert, and stress result — with a cached or templated fallback.

05 Correctness at the data layer

Look-ahead bias structurally prevented, exact portfolio replay via event sourcing, and alert-once-per-condition semantics.

06 Demonstrable end-to-end

Every Core requirement demonstrable on the local Docker Compose stack — cloud-ready by design, demonstrated locally.

06Proposed Solution
An event-driven microservices platform
Eight independently deployable services behind an API gateway, integrated by an Apache Kafka event backbone — each owns one bounded context, one database, one pipeline. Nine deployable units; the three compute-heavy services (Simulation, ML, Risk & TDA) scale and fail independently.
